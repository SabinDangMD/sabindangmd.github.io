This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you interact with our SMS-based communication services (the “Service”). We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Your phone number, name, and any information you voluntarily provide during our communications.

We use the information collected to:

By providing your phone number and engaging with our Service, you consent to receiving SMS messages from us.

  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

6. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required by law.

  • Request corrections or deletion of your data.
